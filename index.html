1Who we are

Last Run: Black Hole Escape (“the Game”) is a mobile game for Android published by Code Qalandar (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is used when you play the Game, why, and the choices you have.

By installing or playing the Game you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

2Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not run our own servers for the Game, and we do not sell your personal information.

Saved only on your device

To keep your progress, the Game stores the following on your phone only. It is never sent to us:

  • Best score, best distance and total coins
  • Shop purchases made with in-game coins (upgrades and trails)
  • Settings such as music and sound on/off, and whether you finished the tutorial

Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes this data permanently.

5Your choices and controls

  • Ad privacy: on most Android phones go to Settings → Google → Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID, or to opt out of personalized ads.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states): use the privacy options button on the Game’s main menu to change your ad consent at any time.
  • Leaderboard: using the leaderboard is optional. You can manage your Play Games profile, its visibility, or delete it in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.
  • Local data: uninstall the Game or clear its storage to erase your progress.

6Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7Security, retention and transfers

Information processed by Google services is protected and kept according to Google’s own policies, and may be processed on servers in other countries. Information saved on your device stays there until you delete it. Because we do not run our own servers, we do not hold copies of your game data.
